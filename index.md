# James Cuénod

Welcome to my portfolio website. I spend my time in the intersection between: *biblical studies* (particularly the Old Testament), *software development*, and *artificial intelligence/machine learning*.

I work in the field of **Bible translation** at [Seed Company](https://seedcompany.com). In addition to fine-tuning ML models and generating drafts for teams who are translating the Bible, I build tools to enhance internal workflows, and I research frontier methods of improving AI draft quality for low-resource languages. In my free-time, I work on **Parabible**. Parabible is a tool for original-language Bible research. I have a PhD in Biblical Theology, specializing in **Old Testament**. My research area is **Sabbath Theology**. To find out more about my work in the biblical studies arena, take a look [here](biblical/).

Since my PhD, I have been working in the Bible translation technology sphere (Clear Bible, Biblica, and Seed Company). You can learn more about my (public) work on software [here](software/). Much of my work is not in public; what is public is often free-time projects. But I maintain a fast and error-tolerant [usfm3](https://github.com/jcuenod/usfm3) parser.

I have been programming for more than 2 decades at this point. A lot of the development I do tends to be at the intersection between my discipline of biblical studies my interest in using computers to make that work better/easier so I guess it's a kind of digital humanities but certainly counts as the **intersection between bible and technology**. I blog infrequently about that intersection [here](https://jcuenod.github.io/bibletech).

I got into Machine Learning (what is known today as "AI") in 2017 through <https://fast.ai> and have been exploring the use of NLP in biblical studies ever since (including presenting a paper on this topic at SBL in 2019). I maintain an online tool that uses a fine-tuned ResNet to clean up document scans (e.g., book chapters and journal articles) to help researchers annotate and search their documents. It automatically rotates skew pages, removes border noise, and binarizes pages, which improves OCR quality. It runs on `Cloudflare` workers & pages, `Supabase`, and `Stripe`. The processing is done on `Docker Swarm` in my homelab using `Machine Learning` and a bunch of vision processing (`OpenCV`) tricks. It's live right now at <https://fixpdfs.com>.
